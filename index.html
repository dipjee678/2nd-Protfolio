§1

Introduction

Most of my favourite problems start with a dataset that is too small, too imbalanced, or too noisy, and a person on the other end who needs to trust the answer.

I study Computer Science & Engineering at American International University–Bangladesh, majoring in Computational Theory. That gives me a foundation in machine learning, NLP, graph theory, data structures & algorithms, object-oriented programming, and discrete mathematics: the theory under the tools.

Note 1Coursework: Machine Learning, NLP, Graph Theory, DSA, OOP, C++, Discrete Mathematics.

My research moves between medical image analysis (classifying enamel caries with transfer learning that copes with heavy class imbalance), intelligent sensing (a cloud-linked, low-cost weather station that forecasts with ML), and quantitative risk (Monte Carlo estimation guided by GARCH-family diagnostics).

Note 2The common thread: make the model honest about its uncertainty, and make its reasoning inspectable.

In summary, I contribute:

  1. Research rigour. I take ideas through to peer-reviewed work, with three IEEE papers accepted in 2026.
  2. Engineering follow-through. I build systems people can use, from Java desktop applications to ML-enabled IoT hardware.
  3. Clear teaching. As an instructor and tutor, I have learned to explain hard ideas simply, a skill that carries over directly to writing papers.
